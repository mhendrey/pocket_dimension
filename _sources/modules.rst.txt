pocket_dimension.vectorizer
---------------------------------

The vectorizer module exposes ``TFVectorizer``, ``TFIDFVectorizer``, and
``BM25Vectorizer``. Each accepts a count-min sketch through ``cms_file``,
which may be a path to a saved sketch or an already instantiated sketch object.
